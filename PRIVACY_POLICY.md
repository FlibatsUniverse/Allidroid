# Privacy Policy

Allidroid does not collect, store on any server, or share your personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, telemetry or advertising.

Allidroid runs inside the Discord app on your device. Your settings and plugin data stay on your device unless you choose to export them. Anything you send through Discord is handled by Discord under [its privacy policy](https://discord.com/privacy/).

Allidroid makes network requests only to do its job:

- GitHub, to download Allidroid updates.
- The Alliance badge API at api.flibat.com, to show badges on profiles. It receives the Discord user IDs of the profiles you view.
- The plugin and theme addresses you choose to install from.
